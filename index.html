From the misty heights of Singhason Hills to the sacred stillness of Haflong Lake — every destination we take you to has been chosen for its depth, not its fame.

01
Diphu
Base · Karbi Anglong

Our home. The cultural heart of Karbi Anglong — alive with markets, music, and the warmest welcomes in all of Northeast India.

02
Singhason Hills
Elevation · Karbi Anglong

Sacred ridgelines with panoramic forest canopy views. Home to rare flora, misty mornings, and age-old Karbi ceremonial grounds.

03
Kaziranga
Wildlife · Assam

UNESCO World Heritage Site. One-horned rhinos, wild elephants, and Bengal tigers in floodplain grasslands — approached in quiet, mindful luxury.

04
Haflong
Lake Country · Dima Hasao

Assam's only hill station. Pristine Haflong Lake, orchid forests, and Dimasa tribal culture wrapped in cloud and stillness.

05
Umrangso
River & Lake · Karbi Anglong

Langhin Lake's turquoise waters, power station history, and river-side camping spots that few outsiders have ever seen.

06
Meghalaya Routes
Living Roots · Northeast

Extended journeys into Meghalaya's living root bridges, sacred groves, and Khasi-Jaintia highlands — accessed from our Karbi base.

Culinary Experiences

A Cuisine the
World Hasn't Found Yet

Karbi and Assamese food is one of India's last truly undiscovered culinary traditions. Rice fermented in bamboo. Smoked pork with foraged greens. River fish slow-cooked in banana leaf. We bring it to the table — and into your hands.

🎋
Bamboo Smoked Dishes

Ancestral cooking inside sealed bamboo tubes — rice, pork, fish, and wild herbs slow-cooked in their own steam and smoke.

🍵
Tea Estate Tasting

Single-estate Assam teas tasted at source, guided by the estate's tea sommelier. Morning ritual elevated to ceremony.

🌊
Riverside Dining

Tables set at the water's edge, freshly caught river fish on banana-leaf platters, and fire-lit evenings with Karbi folk music.

🧑‍🍳
Cooking Workshops

Cook alongside Karbi grandmothers in village kitchens. Learn forgotten techniques, eat what you make, leave with recipes and stories.

🔥

The Fire Has Always Been the Table

In Karbi culture, the hearth is not a cooking device — it is the gathering place of memory, story, and kinship. When you eat with us, you are not just tasting food. You are tasting the land, the season, the hands that know it best.

Every meal we serve is sourced from village farmers, river fishermen, and forest foragers we have known for years. Nothing is imported. Nothing is generic. Everything is alive with place.
